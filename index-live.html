Highlights

What Awaits You at Dhaankolhu Rasdhoo Island

Three ways to settle into the rhythm of the island.

Sunset Swing

As the sky turns gold and pink, settle into an overwater swing strung between two old trees and watch the horizon do the rest.

Island From Above

See the island the way the birds do — turquoise shallows wrapping around the harbor, dhonis lined up at the jetty, and rooftops tucked between the palms.

Snorkeling the Lagoon

Slip beneath the surface and discover why our lagoon is a snorkeler's favorite — clear, calm water and colorful reef life.
