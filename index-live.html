Island Activities

Easy ways to fill your days around the island.

Sharks

Venture to nearby reef channels to spot gentle nurse and reef sharks gliding through turquoise water, a thrilling yet safe wildlife encounter guided by experienced local boatmen.

Snorkeling

Slip into the calm lagoon and drift above vibrant coral gardens, spotting colorful reef fish and gentle rays just steps from shore, with half or full-day sessions available.

Fishing

Head out after sunset with local fishermen for a traditional night fishing trip, learning old techniques and reeling in your own catch beneath a sky full of stars.

Dolphin

Cruise out to open water at golden hour as pods of spinner dolphins leap and play alongside the boat, a lively wildlife encounter guests remember long after the trip ends.

Turtle

Snorkel alongside gentle sea turtles gliding through crystal-clear shallows, an easy, unforgettable encounter with one of the ocean's most graceful creatures right off the island's shore.
